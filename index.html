Message from the President

Welcome to the Rotary Club of Lopez. It is my honor to serve alongside dedicated individuals who share a passion for creating meaningful and lasting change in our communities.

Guided by Rotary's principle of Service Above Self, we continue to implement projects that promote education, health, environmental sustainability, and community empowerment.

Together, let us inspire hope, foster fellowship, and make a positive impact in the lives of others. Thank you for being part of our journey.

Maria Luisa Francisco

RC Premiere President, Rotary Club of Lopez

As District Governor of the Rotary Club of Lopez, I am honored to support the programs, activities, and initiatives that strengthen our commitment to community service.

Through collaboration, transparency, and effective communication, we ensure that every project reflects the Rotary spirit of integrity, fellowship, and service.

Together, we continue to build stronger relationships, foster meaningful partnerships, and create opportunities that positively impact the lives of those we serve.
